Robotics · AI · TurtleBot3 + Webots

Self-Supervised Pretraining for Jet Physics

ML Research · ML4Sci / GSoC 2026 · LorentzParT-JEPA

A full self-supervised stack (JEPA + MAE) on a hybrid Lorentz-equivariant Particle Transformer, testing whether pretraining improves jet tagging on JetClass. The hard-won answer — no accuracy gain — is proven, not just observed.

  • Found a statistics-corrupting padding bug (present in a peer repo): 2× faster, +0.15 AUC
  • A double-dissociation experiment proving the ceiling is input-limited, not representation-limited
  • Information-theoretic bound turning "SSL doesn't help" into "SSL cannot help, here's why"
  • Distributed (DDP) training with a resumable multi-phase experiment pipeline

Chess World Model

ML Research · Latent World Models · CWM

A JEPA-based world model that learns an internal board representation from move sequences alone — never seeing the board — then predicts future states in latent space. Uniquely verifiable: the true board at every ply is known.

  • Action-conditioned latent dynamics (MuZero / EfficientZero-inspired) with an EMA teacher
  • Chess-GPT autoregressive baseline sharing the transformer backbone
  • Streaming Lichess data pipeline over sharded datasets
  • Phase 1 complete — clears the no-information baseline by a wide margin
