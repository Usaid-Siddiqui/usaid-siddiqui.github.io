    LLM-Driven Robotics Controller

    An LLM-in-the-loop controller for a simulated TurtleBot3. Claude chooses high-level motion primitives while a fast 10 Hz classical layer executes them and vetoes unsafe motion — a "brain vs. reflexes" design where safety never depends on the model being correct.

    • Three-layer hierarchy — cognitive, executive, reactive — separated by timescale over a narrow typed interface.
    • Structured tool-use output, prompt caching, and fail-safe-by-construction error handling.
    • Built and validated end-to-end on ROS 2 Humble.

    Self-Supervised Pretraining for Jet Physics

    A full self-supervised stack (JEPA + MAE) on a hybrid Lorentz-equivariant Particle Transformer, testing whether pretraining improves jet tagging on JetClass. The rigorously-established result: no classification-accuracy gain in this regime — proven, not merely observed.

    • Found a statistics-corrupting padding bug — also present in a peer reference implementation — worth ~2× speed and +0.15 AUC once fixed.
    • A double-dissociation experiment showing the accuracy ceiling is input-limited, not representation-limited.
    • Distributed (DDP) training with a resumable, multi-phase experiment pipeline.
  4. 04

    ML Research

    PyTorch · Transformers · JEPA

    GitHub ↗

    Chess World Model

    A JEPA-based world model that learns an internal board representation from move sequences alone — never seeing the board — then predicts future states in latent space. Uniquely verifiable, since the true board at every ply is known and can be decoded from the model's latent.

    • Action-conditioned latent dynamics (MuZero / EfficientZero-inspired) with an EMA teacher.
    • A Chess-GPT autoregressive baseline sharing the transformer backbone.
    • Phase 1 complete — clears the no-information baseline by a wide margin.
